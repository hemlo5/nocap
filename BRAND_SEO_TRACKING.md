# NoCapBLR Brand and Search Tracking

## Canonical entity

- Brand: **NoCapBLR**
- Canonical site: `https://nocapblr.com`
- Tagline: **Before You Go, Know.**
- Market: Bengaluru, Karnataka, India

## Search Console query groups

Track the following groups separately rather than treating generic `no cap` traffic as a direct brand signal:

1. **Exact brand:** `nocapblr`, `no cap blr`.
2. **Brand plus city:** `nocap bengaluru`, `nocap bangalore`, `bengaluru nocap`, `bangalore nocap`.
3. **Discovery intent:** `honest bengaluru place reviews`, `bengaluru local opinions`, `places to visit in bengaluru`.
4. **Named places and areas:** queries for published place and area routes.

Review impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, top landing pages, and branded/non-branded splits monthly. Avoid treating impressions as users or claiming ranking outcomes before Search Console data supports them.
